How to validate a cross-sell test

Association is not causation. The rules above were mined from historical baskets. They describe what customers already did together, not what they would do in response to a new offer. Only a controlled experiment estimates an increment.

Control
Normal product page and basket — no cross-sell prompt for B.
Treatment
Show the consequent product B whenever the antecedent A is added to the basket.
Primary KPI
Attach rate of B among A buyers.

incremental attach rate = P(B | A, treatment) − P(B | A, control)

Secondary metrics

  • Incremental units of B
  • Basket value
  • Revenue
  • Margin

Guardrails

  • Discount cost
  • A conversion (must not fall)
  • Cannibalization
  • Returns

A rule with a huge lift but a tiny reachable audience may be statistically perfect and commercially irrelevant. Size the prize and the discount together, and let the experiment settle the increment.
